## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Header inspection for WAV and FLAC.
- Fixed and silence-aware streaming chunk plans.
- Resume-safe WAV extraction, JSON/CSV manifests, and SVG spectrograms.
- Local-only browser planning demo and offline documentation site.
